Privacy

HairFormula has no account, no servers and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your own device. Read the full privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move my records to a new phone?
Settings > Data > Export backup creates one file with every client, formula and photo. Open the same screen on the new phone and choose Import backup.

I bought Pro and reinstalled the app.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Pro is tied to the account you bought it with.

Is HairFormula Pro a subscription?
No. It is a single one-time purchase that stays yours.

My timer notification arrives late.
On Android 14 and later, allow "Alarms & reminders" for HairFormula in system settings.
