Privacy Policy

HairFormula · Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: HairFormula does not collect any of your data. Everything you enter — clients, colour formulas, photos, prices and stock — is stored only on your own device. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and we cannot see your records.

Who we are

HairFormula ("the app") is a professional tool for hair colourists to record client colour formulas. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the app stores

The app stores the information you choose to enter, entirely in local storage on your device:

  • Client details you type in: name, phone number, birthday, notes, tone preferences and any allergy note.
  • Colour formulas: brands, shade codes, weights, developer volumes and ratios, processing times, service prices and result notes.
  • Photos you capture or select for a client's before/after record.
  • Product stock entries and app settings such as language, theme, salon name and currency.

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export or share it (see "Sharing you initiate" below).

Information we collect: none

We do not operate any server or database for this app. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account or sign-up. There are no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that profile you. The developer has no technical means of accessing what you store in the app.

Device permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera — only to take before/after photos when you tap a photo slot. Images are written to the app's private storage.
  • Choosing an existing photo — handled by the system photo picker (Android) or the photo library picker (iOS), which gives the app only the image you select. The app does not browse or upload your photo library.
  • Notifications — only to alert you when a processing timer you started has finished, and for the client birthday reminders you opt into by entering a birthday. Notifications are generated on your device.

You can decline or revoke any of these in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Sharing you initiate

Some features hand a file to your device's share sheet, at your explicit request: a before/after result card, a client record PDF, or a JSON backup. What happens to that file afterwards depends on the app you send it to and that app's own privacy policy. We are not involved in the transfer and receive no copy.

Purchases

HairFormula Pro is a single, one-time purchase — there is no subscription. Payment is handled entirely by the store you installed the app from: Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone and iPad. We never see or store your payment details. To confirm whether you own Pro, the app asks that store about the purchase status of the signed-in account; this is handled under Google's Privacy Policy or Apple's Privacy Policy respectively. On iPhone and iPad, use of the app is also governed by Apple's standard licence agreement.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can delete an individual client, formula or photo inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data and photos from your device. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf — and no data to recover if you uninstall without a backup.

Your responsibilities as a professional

If you record details about your clients, you are the data controller for that information under laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK. Please handle client records — especially photographs and any health-related note such as an allergy — in line with your local obligations, and obtain your clients' consent where required.

Children

HairFormula is a professional tool intended for adults working in hairdressing. It is not directed at children.
